Things to Do

Things to Do in Southington

Four seasons, endless reasons to get outside.

Parks & Green Space Trails Winter Events

From fresh powder to fall apples, Southington keeps its calendar full. Whether you're a trail lover, a festival regular, or just looking for a green place to spread a picnic blanket, here's where to start.

Parks and Green Space

Town Green — photo pending

Southington Town Green

The heart of the community, the Town Green is a central gathering place for events and get-togethers throughout the year — from seasonal celebrations to community happenings.

Panthorn Park — photo pending

Panthorn Park

A favorite for families, Panthorn Park offers walking trails, a playground, and picnic areas — an easy way to spend a relaxed afternoon outdoors.

Hit the Trail

Farmington Canal Heritage Trail — photo pending

Farmington Canal Heritage Trail

This scenic multi-use trail is perfect for walking and biking, offering an outdoor escape that also connects Southington to neighboring communities. Bring a bike, lace up your sneakers, and see the town from the trail.

Winter Adventures

Mount Southington — photo pending

Mount Southington

When the snow flies, Southington heads for the slopes. Mount Southington is a popular ski resort offering skiing, snowboarding, and snow tubing — a favorite for winter sports enthusiasts of all ages.

Celebrations and Events

Fall

Apple Harvest Festival

Mark your calendar for fall: the annual Apple Harvest Festival is a Southington tradition featuring local food, crafts, and entertainment. It's one of the town's signature events and a great way to experience the community at its best.

Year-round

Local Events Year-Round

Beyond the big annual celebrations, the town hosts community events and gatherings throughout the year that bring neighbors together and keep the community spirit alive.
